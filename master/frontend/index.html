        Alerts are sent by a Telegram bot that you create once. It takes a minute.

        1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather (the official bot, with a blue check).
        2. Send /newbot. It asks for a display name, then for a username that ends in bot.
        3. BotFather replies with a token, something like 123456789:AAH…. Copy it.
        4. Paste it below, press Test, then Save.

        Keep it private: anyone with the token can control the bot.

        Next: open a belt and use Find conversations to choose who receives its alerts.

        Who receives the alerts
        1. On the phone that should receive the alerts, open Telegram.
        2. Search for your bot (the name you chose in BotFather), open it and press Start.
        3. Come back here and press Find conversations, then tick the person.

        For a group: add the bot to the group and send /start@yourbotname in it. Telegram only keeps messages for 24 hours. If someone doesn't show up, ask them to send the message again.
